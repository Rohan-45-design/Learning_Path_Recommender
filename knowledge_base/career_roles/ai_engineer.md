# Career Role: AI Engineer

## Overview
An AI Engineer designs, develops, and deploys scalable intelligent systems leveraging machine learning, deep neural networks, transformers, and generative AI models.

## Required Skill Path
1. Python (Core Programming)
2. Linear Algebra & Machine Learning (Foundations)
3. Deep Learning & Neural Networks (Core AI)
4. Transformers & Attention (Modern Architecture)
5. LLMs & RAG (Production GenAI)

## Industry Demand
High demand across technology, finance, healthcare, and enterprise software industries.
